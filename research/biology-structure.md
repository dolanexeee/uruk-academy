## 4. Print changes / حذوفات — scope decisions (documented, not guessed)

The ministry's curriculum reductions (applied from 2024, reported with
old-print page numbers) removed 42 pages (270 → 228), and this print's
own contents/pages confirm each removal is IN this print:

1. **Ch.5 الوراثة الكمية — DELETED (old pp.222–240).** Confirmed:
   absent from this print's Ch.5 contents (§2). → Out of scope.
2. **Ch.4 التكوين الجنيني للرميح — DELETED in detail (old pp.163–170).**
   Confirmed: no amphioxus section in this print's Ch.4 contents.
   BUT the 2025 دور أول paper still examined «ميزة الأريمة في الرميح»
   because the blastula's features sit inside the chapter's BASIC
   embryology concepts (5-4 مفهوم التكوين الجنيني). → Scope decision:
   the general stages (تفتت → أريمة → معيدة → طبقات جرثومية) are IN;
   the amphioxus-specific development sequence is OUT.
3. **Ch.3 items (old pp.119–132): التكاثر الخضري، التكاثر في الهايدرا،
   التكاثر في البلاناريا، التكاثر في دودة الأرض.** Confirmed by
   sampling this print's pp.112–130: plant reproduction runs
   flower → embryo sac → pollination → fertilization → seed/fruit with
   no vegetative-reproduction section, and animal reproduction runs
   insects → frog → human with NO hydra/planaria/earthworm sections.
   → These four topics are OUT as sections; asexual reproduction types
   remain IN only as the general concept of 3-3 أنواع التكاثر.
4. Some figures became للاطلاع only (رسم الجسيمات الحالة، رسم الأنسجة
   العصبية، رسم الكلاميدوموناس) — text content stays examinable;
   figures themselves are not reproduced anywhere in the platform
   (house rule: original diagrams only).

Print comparison note (the Physics/Chemistry precedents): the chapter
SKELETON (5 chapters, same titles, same order) is stable across the old
270-page print and this 228-page print — what changed is the حذوفات
inside Ch.3/Ch.4/Ch.5, documented above. No chapter merger occurred.

## 6. Terminology & notation discipline

- Terms exactly as the book prints them (Arabic + the book's own
  English in parentheses): الجسيمات الحالة، النبيبات الدقيقة، التمذوب
  is chemistry — biology uses التناضح/الانتشار/النقل الفعال; FSH/LH by
  the book's names (الهرمون المحفز للحويصلات / الهرمون الأصفر);
  النطقة الشفافة (Zona Pellucida); الجسم الأصفر (Corpus Luteum).
- Genetics notation follows the book: alleles as Latin letters in
  KaTeX ($Hb^A$, $Hb^S$, $Cc$), crosses written P₁ → G₁ → F₁ as the
  book lays them out (verified p.190), ratios as plain fractions/
  percentages/ratios (3:1, 9:3:3:1, 1:2:1) with every probability
  re-derived at authoring and again in the cross-check.
- Punnett squares are content tables, not diagrams; the four new
  original SVG diagrams are `cell-schematic`, `flower`,
  `dna-replication`, `pedigree` — drawn from scratch in the platform
  style, never traced from the book.
