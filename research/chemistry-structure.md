## 1. The book: EIGHT chapters — and NO print merger (unlike Physics)

- The textbook's own مقدمة states it verbatim: «يتضمن هذا الكتاب تمهيدًا …
  **ثمانية فصول**» — and the فهرس lists exactly eight. Authors (committee):
  أ.د. عمار هاني الدجيلي، د. سرمد بهجت ديكران، د. سمير حكيم كرمي، ماجد حسني خلف،
  خليل رحيم علي، د. هدى صالح كرمي.
- **Print-to-print check (the Physics lesson applied):** the 10th edition (2024)
  contents and the 9th edition (2023) contents were compared section by section
  for Ch. 1–2 (identical, incl. page-flow of sections) and by chapter titles for
  Ch. 3–8 (identical). No chapter merger, deletion or renumbering between the
  two current prints was found — the merge that hit Physics (10→9) has **no
  chemistry analogue**. All five examined وزاري rounds (2025 ×4 + تمهيدي 2026)
  examine exactly these eight chapters.
- **Scope decision (binding):** build the current book — 8 chapters:

## 4. Constants discipline (the book's / papers' own values)

All computation uses the values the ministry book and papers themselves use;
any problem needing a constant PRINTS it in its statement (book habit):

- $F = 96500\ \text{C/mol}$ (ثابت فاراداي as printed in the papers).
- $R = 0.0821\ \text{L·atm/(mol·K)}$ for $K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}$;
  $R = 8.314\ \text{J/(mol·K)}$ for $\Delta G^\circ = -RT\ln K$ — problems state
  the value they expect.
- $K_w = 1.0\times10^{-14}$ at 25 °C ($pK_w = 14$); $\log 2 = 0.301$,
  $\log 3 = 0.477$, $\log 5 = 0.699$ printed when a problem needs them.
- $1\ \text{cal} = 4.184\ \text{J}$; specific heat of water
  $4.184\ \text{J/(g·°C)}$ (book's table 1-1).
- Atomic masses (printed in problems): H 1 · C 12 · N 14 · O 16 · Na 23 ·
  Mg 24 · Al 27 · S 32 · Cl 35.5 · K 39 · Ca 40 · Fe 56 · Cu 63.5 · Zn 65 ·
  Br 80 · Ag 108 · Ba 137 · I 127 · Pb 207.
- Standard reduction potentials used (V vs SHE): K −2.93 · Na −2.71 ·
  Mg −2.37 · Al −1.66 · Zn −0.76 · Cr −0.74 · Fe −0.44 · Cd −0.40 ·
  Ni −0.25 · Sn −0.14 · Pb −0.13 · H₂ 0.00 · Cu +0.34 · I₂ +0.54 ·
  Ag +0.80 · Br₂ +1.07 · Cl₂ +1.36 · F₂ +2.87 — the subset a problem uses is
  printed in its statement.
- $\Delta H_f^\circ$ / $S^\circ$ / $\Delta G_f^\circ$ data: printed in the
  problem (book-exercise habit); never assumed known.
- Numbers are chosen so roots and logarithms come out clean (the دور أول 2025
  commentary shows the real papers sometimes omit needed roots — a cruelty
  the platform deliberately does not imitate).

## 5. Equations rendering — mhchem decision (documented per commission)

KaTeX's mhchem extension (`katex/dist/contrib/mhchem`, already inside the
installed katex package — no new dependency) was tested headlessly before
authoring: `\ce{H2SO4 + 2NaOH -> Na2SO4 + 2H2O}`, `\ce{N2 + 3H2 <=> 2NH3}`,
state symbols `\ce{H2O(l)}` and precipitation `\ce{SO4^2- + Ba^2+ -> BaSO4 v}`
all render without error. **Decision: mhchem is adopted** — `MathText` imports
it once; because it ships inside the local bundle it is precached by the PWA
service worker and works fully offline, identically to plain KaTeX. Usage
rules are in the authoring spec. A Playwright guard asserts zero
`.katex-error` nodes on the chemistry pages the tests visit. Fallback if any
equation class misrenders: hand-format that equation with `\text{}` +
subscripts (the physics style) — decision recorded here so the cross-check
can enforce it.
